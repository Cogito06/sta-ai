# In Act 1, when should I path into an elite, and how do I fight each one?

Asked by: player

## Current view

Rule of thumb (Ironclad A5): enter an Act 1 elite only above ~60% HP, or ~50% if a rest comes right after AND the deck has a real damage card beyond the starter. Below that, take the ? or monster path even if it pushes the elite later. Expect each Act 1 elite to cost 15-50 HP; two elites back to back need a rest between.
Gremlin Nob: play only attacks (each skill gives it +2 Str; powers like Inflame are safe and best on its turn-1 buff turn). With a near-starter deck it cost me ~45 HP (71 -> 23); with Inflame + Perfected Strike + Pommel/Wild Strike it cost 26 (68 -> 42) in 4 turns. Shockwave still worth playing once (Weak cuts its 24-hit); the fight is a pure damage race.
Lagavulin (110 HP): hitting it while asleep wakes it; in run 4 it attacked 20 the very next turn (no skipped turn), then 20 again, then Siphon Soul (-1 Str/-1 Dex), repeat. Awake it has no Metallicize. Open with all attacks while asleep, dump damage on the Siphon turn, play powers (Combust) early. Even from 62/72 with Combust, Heavy Blade, Pummel it cost 49 HP (ended at 13). Starter-ish deck needs ~5-6 turns.
Sentries (3x ~40 HP, Artifact 1 each): the side two and the middle one take turns between a 10-damage attack and shuffling Dazed into your deck. Kill a side Sentry first with the biggest attacks, then the next. Artifact eats the first debuff (Bash's or Thunderclap's Vulnerable; Clothesline strips it). Combust/Thunderclap chip all three; Explosive Potion helps; Carnage (20) one-shots a damaged Sentry, and Headbutt to put Carnage back on top works since the Dazed clog the draw; Burning Pact exhausting a Dazed is a fine play. Cost: 58 -> 41 (Explosive Potion + Combust), 67 -> 34 in 5 turns (Pommel x2, Carnage, Combust, Thunderclap), 78 -> 43 in 7 turns (starter + Clothesline/Perfected/Pommel/Wild Strike), so go in rested.
Evidence: run 2 (A5, F6 Nob win at 71 HP, F8 Lagavulin loss at 24 HP); eval seed EVAL1 (F6 Sentries 58->41, F8 Lagavulin 62->13); EVAL2 (F7 Sentries 78->43, F10 Nob 68->42, both after a rest); EVAL3 (F13 Sentries 67->34).
