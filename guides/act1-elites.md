# In Act 1, when should I path into an elite, and how do I fight each one?

Asked by: player

## Current view

Rule of thumb (Ironclad A5): enter an Act 1 elite only above ~60% HP, or ~50% if a rest comes right after AND the deck has a real damage card beyond the starter (Perfected Strike alone is not enough). Below that, take the ? or monster path even if it pushes the elite later.
Gremlin Nob: play only attacks (each skill gives it +2 Str). With a near-starter deck it cost me ~45 HP (71 -> 23). Shockwave still worth playing once (Weak cuts its 24-hit), but the Enrage stack grows; the fight is a pure damage race.
Lagavulin (110 HP): hitting it wakes it, it skips that enemy turn, then attacks ~18-20, attacks again, then Siphon Soul (-1 Str/-1 Dex) and repeats. Awake it has no Metallicize. Save Shockwave/Disarm for the attack turns, dump all damage on the Siphon turn. Starter-ish deck needs ~5-6 turns = ~40+ HP lost.
