# At the start of a run, which of Neow's options should I pick, and why?

Asked by: human

## Current view

Ironclad, Ascension 0-5. In order of preference:
1. Trade options: "lose ~8 Max HP, gain 250 Gold" is my first pick. The first shop turns it into a key card, a removal and a potion, which pays off from the first few floors; the lost Max HP comes back later (Singing Bowl, Darkstone, events). Max HP for a rare card or removing/transforming 2 cards is also fine. Skip trades that cost a curse or all your gold unless the reward is a rare relic.
2. Card removal (remove a Strike/Defend) or choosing one of three class cards: a sure deck improvement.
3. Random common relic / Max HP: safe but often near-useless (got Regal Pillow once, which only helps at rests). I now rank it below a removal.
4. Upgrade/transform 1 is weak.
5. Boss relic swap: avoid. At Ascension, losing Burning Blood (6 HP after every fight) is a big risk, and the random boss relic may not fit (Ectoplasm, Runic Dome).
The map can be checked with inspect map on the Neow screen; do it, since the Neow pick should fit the first floors (gold is worth less with no early shop).
Evidence: run 1 (A5 win, F51, score 884): took 250 Gold, bought Immolate, True Grit, a removal and a Blood Potion at the F4 shop; Immolate decided the slime fight, the Sentry elite and Hexaghost. Run 2 (A5 loss, F8): no gold trade offered; took the common relic (Regal Pillow, no effect before death). The loss came from fighting two elites at low HP, not from Neow.
Open questions: is the boss swap worth it at Ascension 0 (energy relics like Fusion Hammer or Sozu)?
