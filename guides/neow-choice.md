# At the start of a run, which of Neow's options should I pick, and why?

Asked by: human

## Current view

Ironclad, Ascension 0-5. In order of preference:
1. Trade options: "lose ~8 Max HP, gain 250 Gold" is my first pick. The first shop turns it into a key card, a removal and a potion, which pays off from the first few floors; the lost Max HP comes back later (Singing Bowl, Darkstone, events). Max HP for a rare card or removing/transforming 2 cards is also fine. Skip trades that cost a curse or all your gold unless the reward is a rare relic.
2. Non-card reward (random common relic, Max HP): safe but small; take it when nothing above is on offer.
3. Card option: removing a Strike or choosing one of three class cards is decent; upgrade/transform 1 is weak.
4. Boss relic swap: avoid. At Ascension, losing Burning Blood (6 HP after every fight) is a big risk, and the random boss relic may not fit (Ectoplasm, Runic Dome).
Evidence: one run only (A5 win, F51, score 884): took 250 Gold, bought Immolate, True Grit, a removal and a Blood Potion at the F4 shop; Immolate decided the slime fight, the Sentry elite and Hexaghost.
Open questions: is the boss swap worth it at Ascension 0 (energy relics like Fusion Hammer or Sozu)? How much is 250 Gold worth when the first floors have no shop - can the map be checked (inspect map) from the Neow screen?
